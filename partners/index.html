For partners

Working alongside the people who care for these communities.

We partner with community health centers, state health departments, and Medicaid health plans to reach patients between visits, especially in rural communities.

Who we work with

One care team, built for each partner.

Community health centers

Reach more of your patients without adding staff.

Send us a list of patients who are due. We handle outreach, testing, and follow-up, and send results back to you.

For health centers →

State health departments

Bring preventive care to rural communities.

We partner with health centers across your state to run screening programs for the people who are hardest to reach, and report on reach, completion, and follow-up.

Talk to our team →

Medicaid health plans

Close screening gaps at home.

At-home colon cancer, diabetes, and kidney screening for hard-to-reach members, documented to your measures.

How a partnership works

Light on your team.

1

You share a patient list

By secure file transfer or API.

2

We reach out for you

Texts go out on behalf of the patient’s health center.

3

Our clinicians do the work

Our physicians order every test and our nurses handle follow-up.

4

You get results and reports

Completed screenings and monthly reporting come back to you.

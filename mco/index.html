For Medicaid health plans

Screening at home for the members hardest to reach.

We help Medicaid plans close colon cancer, diabetes, and kidney health gaps, as a contracted clinical service, not a platform your team has to learn.

Talk to our team

What we help close

Three screenings, documented to your measures.

HEDIS COL

Colon cancer screening

At-home tests for members 45 to 75, with follow-up to colonoscopy when needed.

HEDIS HBD

Diabetes control

At-home A1c testing, with out-of-range results sent to the member’s care team.

HEDIS KED

Kidney health

Annual at-home urine testing for members with diabetes.

Built for your members

Designed for the people traditional outreach misses.

  • Text first. No app, no portal, no letters. Plain language in English and Spanish.
  • Safety check before every kit. A few questions flag members who need a doctor’s visit instead.
  • Follow-up that closes the loop. Abnormal results go to physician review, then the next appointment.
  • No duplicate testing. Members already up to date elsewhere aren’t contacted.

The partnership

What you get, and what we need.

What you get

  • Measure closure for COL, HBD, and KED, in your supplemental data format
  • Monthly reports by product, line of business, and market
  • Flexible contracting, per completion, PMPM, or a mix
  • A full audit trail for every touchpoint and result

What we need

  • A list of members you want us to reach
  • A partnership agreement, including a standard HIPAA agreement
  • A way to receive results that your quality team already uses
  • A main contact on your quality team

Questions

What plan leaders ask us first.

Who is the ordering provider?

Our physicians sign every order and are documented as the ordering provider on every result.

Can we focus on specific members?

Yes. Most partnerships start with a group the plan chooses, such as never-screened members or a specific region.

How fast can we start?

First outreach goes out two to four weeks after signing, with steady outreach by week six.

Does this work alongside our other vendors?

Yes. We check members’ health records first, so nobody already screened is contacted again.

Tell us about your members.

A short call to walk through your priorities, which members to start with, and what results could look like.

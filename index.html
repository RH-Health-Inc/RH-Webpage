How it works

Three simple steps.

  1. We mail you a test. It comes with easy instructions in English or Spanish.
  2. You mail it back. Our doctors and nurses review your result.
  3. We text you what’s next. If you need more care, we help you get it.

Working alongside health centers and states.

We partner with community health centers and state health departments to reach patients between visits, especially in rural communities. Our team handles outreach, testing, and follow-up, and results go back to the health center.

Still have questions?

We’re here to help. Email us at info@robinhoodhealth.co.

Is the text message really from you?

Yes. We text on behalf of your health center. We will never ask for your bank or credit card information by text. If you’re unsure, call your health center.

How do I do the test?

Your kit comes with simple step-by-step instructions. Follow them at home and mail the kit back. Text us anytime with questions.

How do I get my results?

Our clinical team reviews every result. We’ll text you when it’s ready, and our nurses will help you with any next steps.

How do I stop the texts?

Reply STOP at any time.

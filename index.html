A virtual medical group for Medicaid

Preventive care that comes home.

Robinhood Health partners with community health centers and states to bring cancer, diabetes, and kidney screening to Medicaid patients at home, starting in rural America. Our doctors and nurses handle everything, from the test kit to the follow-up.

Why we exist

Care gaps aren’t a motivation problem. They’re a distribution problem.

Getting screened usually means finding an open appointment, taking an unpaid day off, and finding a ride. For people on Medicaid, especially in rural communities, it often never happens. The result is a gap that shows up in every measure, every year.

How it works

We bring the doctor’s office to the patient.

Our partners send us a list of patients who are due. We take it from there and close the loop.

1

A simple test, mailed home

We reach out by text on behalf of the patient’s health center and mail an easy at-home test. No appointment, no day off, no ride.

2

Our doctors and nurses read the results

A licensed physician orders every test and our clinical team reviews every result.

3

We manage the follow-up

Patients get results and next steps by text. If they need more care, our nurses help them get there, and the results go back to their health center.

Who we work with

Built in partnership with the people already caring for these communities.

We don’t replace the local health center. We become a contracted provider alongside it, adding the clinical capacity to reach patients between visits.

Our care model

Doctors and technology under one roof.

We’re a doctor’s office, not a software company. We run the whole process in house, with tools we built to do the busywork so our clinicians can focus on patients.

People

Our clinical care team

Licensed physicians and population health nurses who order every test, review every result, and own every follow-up. They are always in charge.

For patients

Got a text or a test kit from us?

We work on behalf of your community health center to make screenings easier. Here’s what to know.

Who is Robinhood Health?

We’re a virtual medical group that partners with your community health center. Our doctors and nurses help you get the screenings you’re due for, from home.

Is the text message really from you?

Yes. We text on behalf of your health center. We will never ask for your bank or credit card information by text. If you’re unsure, call your health center or email us at info@robinhoodhealth.co.

How do I do the test?

Your kit comes with simple step-by-step instructions. Follow them at home and mail the kit back. Text us anytime if you have a question.

How do I get my results?

A member of our clinical team reviews every result. We’ll text you when it’s ready, and if you need follow-up care, our nurses will help you set it up.

How do I stop the texts?

Reply STOP at any time. You can also reach us at info@robinhoodhealth.co.

This is not for emergencies. If you’re having a medical emergency, call 911. If you’re in crisis, call or text 988.

For partners

Questions we hear most often.

Do we need to integrate with your systems?

No. We pull clinical history through the Carequality and Commonwell national networks and send results back to your EHR. Your only lift is sending us a patient list and receiving results.

Who is the provider of record?

Robinhood Health. Our physicians sign each order, and our nurses manage each abnormal result. This is a contracted care service, not a software tool.

How fast can we start?

Paperwork and BAA in week one, first patient outreach in weeks two through four. Most partners are live within 45 days.

How is patient data protected?

We are fully HIPAA compliant, with a BAA in place with every partner. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is role-based, and every action is logged.

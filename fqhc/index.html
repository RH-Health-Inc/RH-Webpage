For community health centers

Reach more of your patients, without adding staff.

We work alongside your health center as a contracted clinical partner. You send us a list of patients who are due for screening. Our doctors and nurses do the rest, and the results come back to you.

Talk to our team How it works

How it works

Light on your team. Complete for your patients.

1

You send a patient list

Names and contact details, by secure file transfer or API. That’s the main ask.

2

We reach out for you

Patients get a text from us on behalf of your health center, in English or Spanish.

3

Our doctors order, kits go home

A licensed physician reviews each patient before any kit ships.

4

Results come back to you

We follow up on abnormal results and send completed screenings to your EHR for UDS credit.

The partnership

What you get, and what we need.

What you get

  • UDS credit for colon cancer, diabetes, and kidney screenings, documented in your EHR
  • Follow-up that closes the loop, from abnormal result to next appointment
  • Patients who stay yours. Every message comes from us on your behalf
  • Monthly reports on reach, completion, and results

What we need

  • A patient list of the people you want us to reach
  • A Business Associate Agreement, signed in week one
  • One clinical contact for abnormal results
  • Nothing else. No EHR integration, no IT project, no new workflow

Timeline

Live in about six weeks.

Week 1

Paperwork

BAA, data sharing, and secure file transfer set up.

Weeks 2–3

First patient list

We review the first group of patients with our medical director.

Weeks 3–4

First outreach

First texts go out and first kits are in the mail.

Weeks 5–6

Results and reporting

Results flow back and you get your first report.

Questions

What health center leaders ask us first.

Who is the ordering provider?

Our physicians sign every order. Your health center is listed as the patient’s medical home.

Do patients stay with our health center?

Yes. We reach out on your behalf and never replace your primary care relationship.

Do patients pay anything?

No patient receives a bill from us. Preventive screenings in our program are covered at no cost for Medicaid patients.

What if a patient was already screened somewhere else?

We check health records first. Patients who are already up to date won’t be contacted.

How are abnormal results handled?

Our nurses manage every abnormal result, coordinate the next step, such as a colonoscopy, and let your team know.

Tell us about your patients.

A short call to walk through which screenings are open, which sites to start with, and what the first 90 days could look like.

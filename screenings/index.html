What we help with

Screenings that catch problems early.

Today we offer three at-home screenings. Each one is simple to do, and catching problems early makes them much easier to treat.

Colon cancer

For adults 45 and older

An easy at-home stool test. You collect a small sample, seal it, and mail it back.

Colon cancer is very treatable when it’s caught early. If your result needs follow-up, our nurses help you schedule a colonoscopy.

Diabetes

For people living with diabetes

A quick finger-prick test that shows how well your blood sugar has been controlled over the last few months.

If your number is high, we share it with your care team and help you get the support you need.

Kidney health

For people living with diabetes

A simple urine test that checks how well your kidneys are working.

Diabetes can affect the kidneys over time, so a yearly check helps catch changes early.

Not sure if you’re due?

Your community health center knows your history. If you’re due for one of these screenings, you may get a text from us on their behalf.

About us

A doctor’s office for
the people healthcare
reaches last.

Robinhood Health is a virtual medical group. We partner with community health centers and states to bring preventive care home to people on Medicaid, especially in rural communities.

What we believe

Care should fit people’s lives.

The appointment should come to the home.

No day off, no ride, no waiting room. A simple test in the mail.

The conversation should meet people where they are.

Plain language, by text, in English or Spanish.

The result should go back to the patient’s health center.

We work alongside local care teams, never around them.

Why we started

Better software wasn’t enough.

Before Robinhood Health, our founding team built clinical decision support software for doctors’ offices. It was used at more than 180 care sites and was acquired in 2021.

It also taught us something. Health centers serving people on Medicaid didn’t need another tool. They needed more hands to reach patients between visits.

So we became the doctor’s office. We built the technology and the clinical team together, so a small group of doctors and nurses can reach a lot of patients.

“The barrier is almost never the patient. It’s the system’s ability to meet them where they are.”

What we do

Doctors and technology under one roof.

Today we focus on three screenings: colon cancer, diabetes, and kidney health. Our physicians order every test, our nurses handle every follow-up, and two tools we built ourselves do the busywork.

How it works

Screening, made simple.

No appointment, no day off, no ride. We mail you a test, our doctors and nurses review it, and we text you what’s next.

Step by step

From your mailbox to your results.

1

You get a text from us

We reach out on behalf of your community health center to let you know you’re due for a screening.

2

We mail you a test

Your kit comes with simple instructions in English or Spanish. Do it at home, on your schedule.

3

You mail it back

Our doctors and nurses review every result.

4

We text you what’s next

You get your result by text. If you need more care, our nurses help you set it up.

If your result is normal

We’ll let you know by text and remind you when it’s time for your next screening.

If you need follow-up care

A nurse from our team will reach out, explain what it means, and help you get the next appointment. We keep your health center in the loop.

Questions

Got a text or a test kit from us?

Here’s what to know.

Is the text message really from you?

Yes. We text on behalf of your health center. We will never ask for your bank or credit card information by text. If you’re unsure, call your health center or email us at info@robinhoodhealth.co.

How do I do the test?

Your kit comes with simple step-by-step instructions. Follow them at home and mail the kit back. Text us anytime with questions.

How do I get my results?

Our clinical team reviews every result. We’ll text you when it’s ready, and our nurses will help you with any next steps.

How do I stop the texts?

Reply STOP at any time.

This is not for emergencies. If you’re having a medical emergency, call 911. If you’re in crisis, call or text 988.

Still have questions?

Reply to any text from us, or email our team.
